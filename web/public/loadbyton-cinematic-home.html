Before Loadbyton

Every load is an opportunity.
LOAD|BY|TON makes it visible.

Every day, thousands of loads move through businesses, warehouses, ports, industrial areas and supply chains.

But behind every load is an opportunity — for a transporter, a logistics company, a service provider, or a business that needs the right movement at the right time.

The challenge isn't always finding the load. It's making the opportunity visible, structured and actionable.

01 / INTERRUPTION

Every update arrives as another notification.

A driver change, a new transporter reply, a gate slot, a POD request, a rate sheet update — each appears in a different channel and competes for attention.

02 / THREADS

One load becomes five WhatsApp conversations.

The shipper, dispatcher, driver and transporter may each hold a different piece of the same operational story.

03 / FILE CHAOS

The "latest version" changes by the minute.

Spreadsheet, email, POD, rate sheet and call notes overlap on the same desktop. The work is happening — but the context is scattered.

04 / RECONSTRUCTION

Operations ask: what actually changed?

The expensive part is not receiving information. It is rebuilding the job state from disconnected information.
